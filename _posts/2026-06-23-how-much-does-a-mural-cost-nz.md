It's the first question everyone asks, and understandably so. A mural is a meaningful investment — you want to know what you're getting into before you pick up the phone. Here's an honest answer.

## The short answer

Most commercial mural projects in New Zealand fall between **$5,000 and $18,000 +GST**. That's a wide range, and for good reason — murals vary enormously in size, complexity, and what they involve to produce.

A small feature wall in a café might sit at the lower end. A large-scale exterior mural across a building face will be at the top or beyond it.

## What actually drives the cost

**Size** is the biggest factor. Mural pricing is roughly proportional to area — the more wall, the more time, and time is the primary input. A 3m × 3m wall and a 10m × 4m wall are very different propositions.

**Complexity** matters too. A simple typographic design with flat colours is faster to execute than a detailed illustrative scene with fine shading and realistic textures. Both can look excellent — they just take different amounts of time.

**Access** affects cost more than people expect. A ground-level interior wall is straightforward. An exterior wall requiring scaffolding, a boom lift, or working at height adds cost for the equipment hire and the additional time involved in setting up and moving safely.

**Location** plays a small role. Off The Wall is based in North Canterbury and travel within the region is included. Projects outside Canterbury include travel costs in the quote.

## What's included

Every Off The Wall quote is all-inclusive: design, materials, travel (within Canterbury), and painting. There's no separate design fee tacked on afterward. What you're quoted is what you pay — plus GST.

Payment terms are 50% deposit on signing, 50% on completion.

## Why there's no price list

Some clients are surprised there isn't a fixed rate card. The reason is that quoting a flat rate for murals would either be unfair to you (you'd overpay for a simple project) or unworkable for me (I'd underquote a complex one). Every project is different enough that individual quoting is the only honest approach.

What I can do is turn around a rough estimate quickly — usually within 2–3 business days of receiving a photo of the wall, its dimensions, and the location.

## Getting an accurate number

The fastest path to a real quote:

1. Send a photo of the wall or space
2. Include the dimensions (width × height in metres)
3. Note the location
4. Share any design inspiration you have — or tell me you don't have any yet (that's fine)

From there I can give you a rough estimate, and if that works for you, we arrange a site visit to firm it up.
