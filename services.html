What we do

Transforming spaces into places and more beyond the wall.

We design and paint custom murals for commercial spaces. Hospitality venues, retail environments, offices, and anywhere a wall faces the world. Every project is handled end to end: from the first conversation about your space through to the final brushstroke.

Why Off The Wall

What makes the difference

We speak brief

A lot of clients know they want something on the wall but can't quite articulate what. We ask the right questions to draw that out — your brand, your story, your customers, what you want people to feel. The result is a design that makes you say "yes, exactly."

What you see is what you get

Before paint touches your wall, you'll see a scaled digital mockup showing exactly how the finished mural will look in your space. No surprises. No guesswork. Just a clear picture of what you're investing in.

Design and painting, one person

The same person who interprets your brief is the one holding the brush. That continuity means nothing gets lost between concept and execution and you have one point of contact throughout.
